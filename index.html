What is Mintrage?

Your business.Your customers.Your price.Our production.

Mintrage enables students, employees and aspiring entrepreneurs to build digital, social-media-based businesses without maintaining inventory or building their own production team.

Sellers choose services from the Mintrage Catalogue, promote them through their own business channels, set their own selling prices and acquire their own customers.

When a seller receives an order, they submit it to Mintrage. After payment confirmation, Mintrage produces the deliverable and sends it digitally to the seller. The seller then delivers it to their customer.

Mintrage is your production and fulfillment partner. We do not find customers for you.

How it works

  1. Register as a seller.
  2. Choose services from the catalogue.
  3. Set your own selling price.
  4. Find customers.
  5. Pay the applicable Mintrage base price and submit the order.
  6. Receive the completed deliverable, deliver it to your customer and keep the difference between your selling price and the base price.

Your gross profit before other expenses = Customer selling price − Applicable Mintrage base price

This is not guaranteed net income. Marketing, payment fees and other costs come out of it.
