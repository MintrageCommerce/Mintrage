Frequently Asked Questions

General

What is Mintrage Commerce?

Mintrage Commerce enables students, employees and aspiring entrepreneurs to build digital businesses without maintaining inventory or building their own production team. Sellers promote and sell listed Mintrage products/services while Mintrage handles the production and fulfillment.

How does Mintrage Commerce work?

You register, choose what you want to sell, promote your business, find customers, collect payment from your customers, pay the applicable Mintrage base price, submit the order and receive the completed deliverable from Mintrage. You then deliver it to your customer and keep your markup.

Where can I sell Mintrage products/services?

You can promote your business through social media platforms, social media shops/pages, your personal network, communities, workplace or other channels where you are permitted to conduct business.

Can I sell selectively?

Yes. You can choose any of the products/services listed in the Mintrage Commerce Catalogue. You do not have to sell everything.

Does Mintrage guarantee sales or income?

No. Mintrage Commerce does not find customers for sellers and does not guarantee sales, customers or income. Registration does not guarantee that you will receive customers or orders.

Registration

How do I register?

Click the Register Now button and submit the registration form. After you submit it, we will email you the Seller Agreement and payment details within 48 hours. Make the registration payment (USD 25) to the designated payment address and send us back a signed copy of the Seller Agreement. After we verify your payment, you will receive a confirmation email of your successful registration. It may contain:

  • Confirmation of registration
  • The applicable catalogue of services/products
  • The applicable Mintrage base prices
  • Initial marketing materials
  • The executed/signed Seller Agreement
  • Supplementary documents and files
  • Instructions regarding future order submission
  • The designated order-submission email address or other applicable channel
  • Other information required for the seller to begin operating

Once you register and make your payment you can begin selling our services/products and we will produce them for you.

How much is the registration fee?

The annual registration fee is USD 25 and is valid for 12 months.

Is the registration fee refundable?

No. The annual registration fee is non-refundable.

How long does registration verification take?

Registration is generally reviewed within 1–2 days. In certain circumstances, verification may take up to 7 days.

Can someone else pay my registration fee?

Yes. Payment may be made from a bank account or Mobile Financial Services account that is not in your own name. You must provide the relevant payment details and proof of payment through email after you have made the payment.

What happens after registration?

Once your registration has been verified, you will receive an email confirmation and access to the relevant seller resources, including the seller catalogue, applicable base prices and delivery information, order submission process, support contact and marketing materials.

Pricing & Profit

Can I add my own profit markup?

Yes. You are free to determine your own customer-facing selling price by adding a meaningful profit markup to the applicable Mintrage base price, subject to applicable laws, platform rules and Mintrage Commerce policies.

Your Profit = Your Selling Price − Mintrage Base Price

Who sets the selling price?

You determine the price at which you sell to your customer.

Who collects payment from the customer?

You do. Mintrage Commerce does not collect payment directly from your customers.

Can I take partial payment or advance payment from my customer?

Your customer payment arrangements are your responsibility. You may determine whether to collect payment in advance, partially in advance or through another arrangement, subject to applicable laws and your own business practices.

Orders & Fulfillment

When do I pay the Mintrage base price?

Once you receive a confirmed customer order, you pay the applicable Mintrage base price and submit the required order information through the Mintrage Commerce order form. Proof of payment is required.

How do I place an order?

After registration, you will receive access to the designated Mintrage Commerce order form. You will submit the customer order details, applicable service information and proof of payment through that form.

Who produces the product/service?

Mintrage Commerce handles the production/creation and fulfillment of the listed digital products/services ordered by registered sellers.

Who delivers the final product/service to the customer?

Mintrage Commerce delivers the completed deliverable to the registered seller. The registered seller then delivers it to their customer.

How long does fulfillment take?

Each service has an estimated minimum and maximum delivery period listed in the catalogue. Delivery timelines may vary depending on the service, order requirements and revisions.

Can an order be cancelled?

If an order is cancelled within 24 hours of ordering, the base price is refundable. See the Refund/Cancellation Policy.

Does Mintrage communicate with my customer?

No. The seller owns and manages the customer relationship. Mintrage Commerce communicates with the registered seller regarding fulfillment.

Seller Responsibilities

What am I responsible for?

You are responsible for promoting your business, finding customers, communicating with customers, setting your selling price, collecting customer payments, providing accurate order information, managing customer expectations and delivering completed products/services to your customers.

What is Mintrage responsible for?

Mintrage Commerce is responsible for producing/fulfilling the listed products/services ordered by registered sellers according to the applicable service specifications.

Eligibility & Restrictions

Are there restrictions on who can register?

Mintrage Commerce may restrict registration from certain countries, territories, individuals, entities or jurisdictions due to applicable laws, sanctions, banking requirements, compliance considerations or internal policies.

Eligibility requirements may change from time to time. If you are unsure about your eligibility, contact Mintrage Commerce before making the registration payment.

I am unsure whether I am eligible. What should I do?

Contact Mintrage Commerce at mintrage.commerce@gmail.com before making your registration payment.
