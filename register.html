Register as a seller

The annual registration fee is US$25, valid for 12 months.

How registration works

  1. Submit this form. Your registration is created as pending.
  2. We email you the Seller Agreement and payment details within 48 hours.
  3. You pay the US$25 fee to the designated payment address and return your signed agreement.
  4. We verify your payment and approve your registration. Submitting the form or claiming payment does not activate your account.
  5. You get your activation email and can log in to the seller portal.

The fee is non-refundable once approved, subject to applicable law. If we decline your registration, you are refunded within 10 to 15 days. See the Refund and Cancellation Policy. Registration does not guarantee sales or income.
