Privacy Policy

What we collect and why.

Version 1.0 draft. Highlighted items [like this] are placeholders that need your confirmation. This document should be reviewed by a qualified legal adviser before launch.

In short: we collect and store the information you give us when you register and afterwards, so that we can run our service smoothly.

What we collect

  • Registration: full name, email address, country of residence, country of business operation, business or social media page (if you give one), and your acceptance of our agreements.
  • Account: your login email and a securely hashed password, account status and registration dates.
  • Payments: the payment reference and any proof of payment you send us. We never ask for card numbers, banking passwords or PINs.
  • Orders: the service you order, your requirements, links and files you provide, and your own order reference.
  • Technical data: basic server logs such as IP address and browser type, used for security and fault-finding.

Why we use it

To register and verify sellers, take and fulfil orders, verify payments, send you service emails, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations and support you.

Who can see it

  • Mintrage administrators.
  • People who produce your deliverable, who see the order details they need.
  • Service providers that host our website and database ([Cloudflare, confirm]) and send our emails ([email provider, confirm]).
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

Cookies and tracking

The public pages do not use advertising or analytics trackers. When you log in, we use an essential session cookie to keep you signed in.
