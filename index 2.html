What is Mintrage Commerce?

Mintrage Commerce is a platform that enables students, employees and aspiring entrepreneurs to build digital, social-media-based businesses without maintaining inventory or building their own production team at near zero capital.

Choose digital products/services from the Mintrage Commerce Catalogue, promote them locally or internationally through your social media business page, personal network or other channels, add your own profit markup, find customers and collect payment directly from them.

Once a customer order is confirmed, Mintrage Commerce handles the production and fulfillment of the selected product or service and delivers the completed digital deliverable to you. You then deliver it to your customer.

How to Register

Getting started takes just a few steps.

  1. Review the Catalogue

    Review the available products/services and decide what you want to sell.

  2. Submit Your Registration Form

    Click the Register Now button and complete the Mintrage Commerce registration form accurately and completely. We will then email you the Seller Agreement and payment details.

  3. Pay the Annual Registration Fee

    Annual Registration Fee: USD 25 · Validity: 12 months · Status: Non-refundable

    Pay the annual registration fee of USD 25 at the designated payment address given in the email sent to the address you provided, and return a signed copy of the Seller Agreement.

  4. Verification

    Registration is generally reviewed within 1–2 days, but verification may take up to 7 days in certain circumstances.

  5. Start Selling

    Once approved, you will receive your seller resources and can begin promoting the listed products/services through your own business channels. Please check your email after registration, as all communication to you will be done there.

Before You Register

Mintrage Commerce provides production and fulfillment services to registered sellers. Sellers are independently responsible for acquiring customers, communicating with customers, determining their selling prices, collecting customer payments and delivering completed products/services to their customers.

Mintrage Commerce does not guarantee sales, customers or income. Your earnings depend on your selling price, customers, sales volume and business activity.
