Refund and Cancellation Policy

When fees are and are not refunded.

Registration fee

  • The US$25 annual registration fee is non-refundable once your registration is approved, subject to applicable law.
  • If Mintrage declines your registration, you are refunded within 10 to 15 days.
  • If you end your registration voluntarily, the fee is not refunded.

Order base price

  • Once you submit an order and Mintrage accepts it, you pay the applicable base price shown in the catalogue.
  • The base price is non-refundable once paid. This includes cancelling within 24 hours of ordering, unless Mintrage expressly agrees otherwise or the law requires it.
